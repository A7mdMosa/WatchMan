# WatchMan Support

WatchMan shows every Claude Code session on your Mac beside the notch, and WatchMan for iPhone lets you see and
answer them from anywhere.

## Get started

1. Download **WatchMan for Mac** (free) from [Releases](https://github.com/A7mdMosa/WatchMan/releases/latest), drag
   it to Applications and open it.
2. In its setup checklist, click **Install** next to "Connect to Claude Code".
3. On the iPhone, install **WatchMan: Session Monitor** and sign in to iCloud with the same Apple ID as the Mac.
4. That's it: the link turns on by itself. WatchMan's Settings → iPhone on the Mac shows "Link through iCloud: On",
   and your sessions appear on the iPhone within a few seconds.

## Common questions

**The iPhone says "Not linked to your Mac yet".** Make sure WatchMan for Mac is running (its Settings → iPhone should
say "Link through iCloud: On") and both devices use the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on. A Mac that's asleep can't send updates.

**No notifications on the iPhone.** Allow notifications for WatchMan in the iPhone's Settings. In WatchMan for Mac,
check Settings → iPhone, and that quiet hours aren't on.

**The Live Activity doesn't show.** Open the app once while a session works or waits, and check that Live Activities
are allowed for WatchMan in the iPhone's Settings.

**Allow on a notification asks for Face ID.** That's on purpose: approving a command from the lock screen needs you
to unlock first.

**Does WatchMan send my code anywhere?** No. Your sessions travel only between your Mac and your iPhone, through your
own private iCloud database. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
